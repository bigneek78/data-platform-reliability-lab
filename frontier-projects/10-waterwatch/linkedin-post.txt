WaterWatch: Pressure Anomaly Review

Which synthetic pressure readings deserve review when sensors can be missing or unusually noisy?

I’m sharing a self-contained Project Phoenix prototype with working code, eight local tests, a reproducible synthetic fixture, an architecture diagram, and an animated execution trace.

The synthetic sequence contains two low-pressure readings marked REVIEW, one UNKNOWN reading, and eight observed readings. Tests check the zero-variation calibration case, a single extreme calibration point, exact threshold equality, negative measurements, and insufficient baseline evidence.

Engineering judgment: The score is a deviation from this baseline, not a probability of a leak and not a potability assessment. A changed pump regime or sensor fault could produce the same pattern. Baseline selection and threshold calibration require field-specific evidence.
