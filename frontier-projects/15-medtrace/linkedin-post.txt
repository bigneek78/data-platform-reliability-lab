MedTrace: Synthetic FHIR Bundle Preflight

Can a small preflight catch duplicate identities and unresolved references before a synthetic bundle enters a pipeline?

I’m sharing a self-contained Project Phoenix prototype with working code, eight local tests, a reproducible synthetic fixture, an architecture diagram, and an animated execution trace.

The synthetic two-entry fixture passes the subset and resolves one reference. Tests intentionally introduce duplicate fullUrls, duplicate resource identities, an unresolved subject, a missing status, and an unsupported resource type. A URN reference is also checked for successful local resolution.

Engineering judgment: This is not a full FHIR validator, server, terminology service, conformance statement, privacy control, or clinical-quality check. The simplified fullUrl and ID rules are narrower than the complete specification, and many required semantic constraints are outside scope. No real patient records are used.

Built with AI assistance. These are synthetic prototype results, not production or employment outcomes.
