RouteRelay: Road Closure Routing

Which route remains available when a road segment closes?

I’m sharing a self-contained Project Phoenix prototype with working code, eight local tests, a reproducible synthetic fixture, an architecture diagram, and an animated execution trace.

The open synthetic road network has a cost-6 route. Closing edge e4 changes the chosen route to Depot, A, Clinic at cost 7. Tests also verify a disconnected depot, the zero-cost same-node case, and rejection of negative edge weights or unknown closures.

Engineering judgment: The model uses static additive cost units, not live traffic or guaranteed arrival times. It does not account for turn restrictions, truck clearances, weather, or emergency priorities. Directed edges are intentional: reversing an edge requires separate source data.

Built with AI assistance. These are synthetic prototype results, not production or employment outcomes.
