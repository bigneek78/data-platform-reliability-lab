EdgeBrake: Latched Robot Watchdog Simulation

When should a host-side simulator latch a stop after stale telemetry or an emergency-stop event?

I’m sharing a self-contained Project Phoenix prototype with working code, eight local tests, a reproducible synthetic fixture, an architecture diagram, and an animated execution trace.

The six-event demonstration produces READY, READY, STOP, STOP, STOP, READY. It records three stopped and three ready events. Boundary tests cover exact age equality, stale resets, healthy resets, time reversal, invalid controls, and an empty stream.

Engineering judgment: This is a host simulation, not a safety-rated controller. Event timestamps and sensor ages are supplied by the fixture rather than a trusted monotonic clock. No actuator, emergency circuit, real-time scheduler, or physical stopping-distance measurement is connected.
