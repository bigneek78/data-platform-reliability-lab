TransitPulse: Bus Headway Holding Simulation

Can bounded holding reduce departure bunching without hiding passenger delay?

I’m sharing a self-contained Project Phoenix prototype with working code, eight local tests, a reproducible synthetic fixture, an architecture diagram, and an animated execution trace.

For the fixture, headway error falls from approximately 6.455 to 1.528 minutes, while the policy adds 20 total vehicle-minutes of hold. The expected departure sequence is asserted directly. Tests also check zero holding, a single bus, empty data, and invalid arrival ordering.

Engineering judgment: This is a terminal policy demonstration, not a city-network optimization. Passenger counts, transfer reliability, dwell time, traffic feedback, driver constraints, and downstream stops are absent. The measured improvement applies to this synthetic arrival sequence only.

Built with AI assistance. These are synthetic prototype results, not production or employment outcomes.
