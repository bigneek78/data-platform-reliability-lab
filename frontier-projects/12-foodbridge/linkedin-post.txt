FoodBridge: Perishable Inventory Allocation

Can a food-rescue desk fulfill timed requests while keeping expired lots out of allocations?

I’m sharing a self-contained Project Phoenix prototype with working code, eight local tests, a reproducible synthetic fixture, an architecture diagram, and an animated execution trace.

The demonstration fulfills 16 requested units, leaves 1 unit, and reports that remaining unit as expired at the reporting horizon. Tests reconcile supply and demand, verify expiry and availability boundaries, and prove that a punctuation-heavy identifier is treated as data rather than SQL.

Engineering judgment: This is a desk allocation model, not a food-safety certification or delivery optimizer. Food types, allergens, handling temperature, nutrition, vehicle routes, and recipient preferences are absent. First-expiring-first-out is not a global proof of minimum waste across all possible future requests.

Built with AI assistance. These are synthetic prototype results, not production or employment outcomes.
