GridIsland: Reserve-Constrained Microgrid Dispatch

When should an idealized battery charge or discharge while retaining a stated reserve?

I’m sharing a self-contained Project Phoenix prototype with working code, eight local tests, a reproducible synthetic fixture, an architecture diagram, and an animated execution trace.

The fixture dispatch costs 36 units versus 57 with no battery operation, and ends at state of charge 2. Tests verify each energy-balance equation, reserve and rate bounds, terminal state, a tiny independently known optimum, and explicit failure when grid limits make service impossible.

Engineering judgment: The model assumes ideal 100-percent battery efficiency. It excludes degradation, conversion losses, forecasts, ramping limits, outages, and power-flow constraints. The cost difference is an algorithm demonstration, not a forecast of a real site savings or a purchasing recommendation.
