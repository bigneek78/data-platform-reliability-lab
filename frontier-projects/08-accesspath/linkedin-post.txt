AccessPath: Constraint-Aware Walking Routes

Which synthetic walking route meets a stated width and slope constraint while avoiding stairs?

I’m sharing a self-contained Project Phoenix prototype with working code, eight local tests, a reproducible synthetic fixture, an architecture diagram, and an animated execution trace.

The fixture rejects the direct stairs and one steep ramp, then selects Entry, Lift, Exit with length 17. Raising the slope limit allows a length-12 alternative. Tests cover exact constraint boundaries, unreachable routes, unknown nodes, and negative lengths.

Engineering judgment: The configuration describes user-selected constraints rather than a legal accessibility standard or a guarantee that a physical route is safe. The graph omits surface condition, crosswalk timing, lift outages, temporary obstructions, and the measurement uncertainty of width and slope.

Built with AI assistance. These are synthetic prototype results, not production or employment outcomes.
